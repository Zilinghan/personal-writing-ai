To the Admissions Committee:
I am a Senior Computer Scientist in the Data Science and Learning Division at Argonne National Laboratory, where I lead PALISADE-X, a project building secure federated learning infrastructure so that hospitals can train clinical AI models together without sharing patient data. Ashish Agrawal joined my group in June 2026 as a high school junior. This letter reflects both my assessment and that of Zilinghan Li, the machine learning engineer in my group who has supervised his work closely.
What first distinguished Ashish is that he finds his own research questions. Rather than waiting to be assigned a project, he proposed one drawn from obstacles he had hit in his own clinical research: a way for multiple hospitals to check whether a clinical model is well calibrated across demographic subgroups without pooling patient records. He then built it, validated it, and integrated it into APPFL, our open-source federated learning framework, submitting a well-tested contribution that is now under review. Few of the undergraduate researchers I have supervised reach that point.
Just as important is how he works. He looks for weaknesses in his own results and reports them unprompted. When Zilinghan identified a privacy flaw at the core of his design, he did not defend it; he fixed it within weeks. He also learns unfamiliar ground quickly: asked to explore whether AI agents could automate parts of our data readiness workflow, he delivered a working prototype in nine days, with the judgment to keep a human in the loop.
Ashish is already a working researcher, with two years of clinical machine learning work. He has reached this point largely by teaching himself; what he needs now is formal training and people who will challenge him, and he will make full use of both.
I recommend him without reservation, and I expect to keep working with him wherever he enrolls. I am glad to answer any questions.
Sincerely,
Ravi K. Madduri Senior Computer Scientist, Data Science and Learning Division Argonne National Laboratory madduri@anl.gov
