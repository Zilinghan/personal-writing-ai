Subject: Sharing Globus Compute Endpoints Across a Federation

===

Hi Ryan,

We have recently been exploring a new way of "sharing" Globus Compute endpoints for running federated learning, and we would love to get your thoughts on it before we try it in a real deployment.

Previously, for a federation (federated learning group, i.e., a group of people or institutions), we had two ways to share endpoints within the group:

(i) Invite everyone in the federation to a Globus group, and then ask Globus support to share each endpoint with that group. This is a legacy solution from about three years ago.
(ii) Use a multi-user Globus Compute endpoint. This is the official solution, but it requires root access on the machine where the endpoint runs.

Now we are wondering whether the following would work:

1) APPFL creates an APPFL-specific Globus account.
2) For each federation, we create a service account under that APPFL-specific account.
3) We then obtain the client ID and client secret for that federation-specific service account.
4) All clients create their own Globus Compute endpoints using that client ID and client secret, which can be shared among the clients through a web dashboard.
5) With these steps, all client endpoints would belong to the federation-specific service account and be accessible across the federation for running federated learning experiments.

I would appreciate your thoughts on this approach. I have also copied my colleagues who may plan to use it.
