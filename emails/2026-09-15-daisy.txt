Subject: Follow-up on ADKO Integration into APPFL

===

Hi All,

This is Zilinghan Li, an engineer at Argonne leading the development of APPFL. I wanted to follow up on the integration of ADKO into APPFL that we discussed in our last meeting.

We have now completed the integration in a working branch of APPFL:
https://github.com/APPFL/APPFL/tree/daisy/prototype

The prototype supports three communication modes: (i) serial mode in a single process for easy setup; (ii) MPI mode for large-scale testing; and (iii) gRPC mode for distributed, decentralized deployment. All three modes have been tested and are working.

John and I are now in the final stage of verification, reproducing the results of the original ADKO repository both with and without LLM usage (we are currently working on the scientific_discovery example). If you have any suggestions on how to set up another simple reproduction test, we would greatly appreciate them.

With the integration nearly complete, we would also like to discuss what the best next steps would be for our collaboration on moving DAISY forward. Thanks a lot!

Best,
Zilinghan
