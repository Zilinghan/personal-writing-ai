Statement of Accomplishments - Zilinghan Li

TIME PERIOD:  October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2025 (FY25)
Summary
During fiscal year 2025, my work primarily focused on advancing federated learning (FL) and developing AI solutions for biomedicine and general science. In FL, I continued to work as the lead developer for the Advanced Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning (APPFL) framework, with new features integrated for a broader application range within the scope of AI for science. In AI for biomedicine and general science, I collaborated with researchers from the University of Pennsylvania and Stony Brook University to build AI-powered tools that address critical challenges in biomedicine. I was also actively involved in the evaluation and post-pretraining teams for the AuroraGPT effort.
