Statement of Accomplishments - Zilinghan Li

TIME PERIOD:  October 1, 2023 through September 30, 2024 (FY24)
Summary
During fiscal year 2024, my work primarily focused on two key research areas: federated learning (FL) and large language models (LLMs). In FL, I led the development and maintenance of the Advanced Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning (APPFL) framework. I also contributed to several FL-related research projects, with multiple papers accepted or submitted to top-tier machine learning and distributed computing conferences. In LLMs, I collaborated with the University of Pennsylvania Medical School to develop a model for screening patients with rare genetic aortopathy. Additionally, I served as the liaison between the post-pretraining and evaluation teams for the AuroraGPT effort.
